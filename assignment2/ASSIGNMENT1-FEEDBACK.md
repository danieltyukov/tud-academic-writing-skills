# Assignment 1 Feedback

**Overall:** Good research question.

## Feedback Point 1: RQ does not cover disadvantages

The RQ mentions improved signal integrity and reduced electromagnetic coupling as advantages, but the motive also promises analysis of potential disadvantages (thermal issues, nanoTSV parasitics, wafer warpage). These disadvantages don't fit into the current RQ.

**Action:** Broaden the RQ to include the trade-off / disadvantage angle. Options:
- *"To what extent does backside power delivery improve signal integrity in advanced CMOS nodes, and what new challenges does it introduce?"*
- *"How do the signal integrity benefits of backside power delivery compare against its new design trade-offs in advanced CMOS nodes?"*

## Feedback Point 2: Reference 2 citation format

Reference 2 (Prasad et al.) has more than six authors (like Reference 1), so it should use "et al." instead of listing all authors.

**Action:** Change to:
> D. Prasad *et al.*, "Buried power rails and backside power grids: Arm CPU power delivery network design beyond 5nm," ...
