# The Results Section

The results section presents the outcomes of your study. More importantly, it presents the evidence for supporting your conclusion. Thus, it is important that there is a clear link between your research question and results.

## How to write a results section

- Read Silyn-Roberts (2013) Chapter 2 - The Core Chapter: Sections and elements of a document, p. 37 (results section in general)
- Read Silyn-Roberts (2013) Chapter 6 - A Journal paper, p. 96 (results section in journal papers)
- See the Academic Phrasebank (University of Manchester) for example sentences for the results section
