# Assignment 3: Body Part of Literature Review (Writing Group)

**Group:** Writing group 7.3
**Feedback:** Peer feedback (Assignment 4)

---

## Task Description

1. **Improve** introduction and method section based on Assignment 2 feedback
2. **Write the body part** of your literature review, or at least a significant part (~1000 additional words)
   - Can be a standard Results section (AIMRaD structure) or self-chosen structure
3. **Focus areas:**
   - Paragraph structure and use of references
   - Clear storyline (helps reader follow your line of thought)
   - Clear and convincing arguments (argumentation is vital — aim to convince reader of your conclusion)
   - Include a visualisation (e.g., a table summarising results — helps reader understand difficult concepts and gives quick overview of important results)

## Writing Process Note

At this stage, focus on **higher-order processes** (structure, argumentation, storyline), not local-level spelling/grammar. Writing does not need to be perfect — may contain unfinished sentences, rough ideas, etc., as long as you keep writing. Let writing go hand-in-hand with thinking.
