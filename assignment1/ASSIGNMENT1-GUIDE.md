## Three Parts

Assignment 1 consists of three parts:

### Part 1a: Research question and scientific gap (motive)

- Briefly describe the **topic** of your literature review paper
- Formulate the **(preliminary) research question** you will answer
- Describe the **scientific gap** (motive) that you will occupy by answering this question

**Motive types** — why is the research question important?
- There is something **unknown**
- There are **conflicting results**
- There is a specific **problem that needs a solution**

### Part 1b: Writing plan

- Describe how you plan to **structure your paper** (preliminary outline)
- Describe if (and how) you **used generative AI** during the orientation phase
- Describe how you **intend to use generative AI** during the rest of the research process
- **Tip:** which sub-questions do you need to answer before you can answer your main question? Map sub-questions to sections.

### Part 1c: Annotated bibliography

- Compile an annotated bibliography of **1 source per person** in the writing group
- Sources should be potentially useful for your literature review paper
- For each source:
  - Give a correct reference in **IEEE style**
  - Summarise by describing the **research question, motive, and conclusions**
  - Briefly explain how you **intend to use this source** in your paper

## Structure Template

Map your sub-questions to paper sections:

```
- Abstract          → MAIN question
- Introduction      → Background sub-questions + MAIN
- Background        → Foundational/definitional sub-questions
- Method & Results  → Core analytical sub-questions
- Discussion        → Evaluation/ethics/limitations sub-questions
- Conclusion        → Summary sub-questions + MAIN
- References
```

---

## Annotated Bibliography Format

For each source, provide:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Reference** | Full citation (use IEEE format) |
| **Motive** | Why was this research done? What gap does it address? |
| **Objective** | What is the specific research question/aim of this paper? |
| **Conclusion** | What did the authors find/conclude? |
| **Argumentation** | Key arguments/evidence (numbered list of main points) |
| **Help** | How does this source help YOUR review? Which sub-question does it address? |

## Research Question Guidelines (from Class 1 + Brightspace)

Your research question should:
- **Lead to a critical review**, not only a descriptive summary of the literature
  - Not: "What are non-invasive human activity recognition and vital signs monitoring methods?"
  - But: "Which challenges can we discover by comparing traditional DSN approaches with novel radar methods for HAR, focusing on applications, intrusiveness, acceptance and privacy?"
- Be neither too broad nor too narrow
- Be clear (no ambiguous words)
- Not be too long
- Be ONE question only
- Start with a wh-word (what, why, when, who, where, how, to which extent)
- Cover everything that will be discussed in the text
- Be achievable (given available time/means)
- Lead to debate
